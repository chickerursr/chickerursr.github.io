01 · About

Researcher · Educator · Mentor

I am a Professor at KLE Technological University and Director of the Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research. I am an NVIDIA DLI Certified Instructor and Ambassador, bringing cutting-edge GPU programming knowledge to learners across India and beyond.

My work spans high-performance computing, deep learning, quantum machine learning, and applied AI — from theoretical research to hands-on industry training at IIT Bombay, IIT Palakkad, NIT Calicut, and international venues in China and the USA.

I am the founder of TechXcelerate Innovations Pvt. Ltd. — an AI-focused startup specialising in healthcare AI, quantum computing, and intelligent product design.

TechXcelerate Innovations bridges cutting-edge academic research with real-world AI product development. Specialising in healthcare AI, quantum computing applications, and intelligent product design, leveraging deep expertise in accelerated computing to build solutions with tangible impact.

Medical Device Quality & Audit Services

Through TechXcelerate Innovations, Prof. Chickerur, a trained ISO 13485:2016 Internal Auditor, supports medical-device manufacturers and health-tech startups on quality-management and risk-management compliance, with a focus on AI and software-based medical devices.

  • Internal audits of quality management systems against ISO 13485:2016
  • QMS gap assessments and audit-readiness reviews ahead of certification audits
  • Supplier (second-party) audits on behalf of client organisations
  • Risk-management support as per ISO 14971:2019, including for AI/ML-based Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)
